💡 What Is candela?

candela is a user behavior analytics tool. In plain language: it records what people do inside a product or website — the buttons they press, the pages they visit, the moment they sign up or leave — and then turns those records into easy-to-read charts and reports.

Most analytics services work by sending your visitors' information to a company's cloud. candela does the opposite. It is self-hosted, which means it runs on a computer that you control, and the data stays there. Nobody else can read it, sell it, or train on it.

Under the hood, candela is built on ClickHouse, a very fast database for large amounts of data, and ClkLog, an open-source analytics foundation. You do not need to understand any of that to use it — it simply means the tool stays quick even when you are storing millions of events.

📊 What You Can Do With It

  • Track events. See exactly which actions people take — clicks, sign-ups, purchases, searches — and how often each one happens.
  • Build funnels. Follow a journey step by step and find the exact point where people give up, so you know what to fix first.
  • Measure retention. Learn how many people come back tomorrow, next week, or next month, and which features keep them returning.
  • Read dashboards. Arrange your most important numbers on a single screen and check it at a glance each morning.
  • Keep everything private. Your data lives on your own machine, which makes candela a good fit for teams with strict privacy or compliance needs.
  • Stay in control with the AGPL-3.0 license. The source code is open, so you can inspect it, change it, and be confident about what it does.

🖥️ System Requirements

candela is comfortable on a normal modern Windows computer. Plan for roughly the following:

  • Operating system: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).
  • Memory: 8 GB of RAM or more recommended.
  • Storage: 10 GB of free disk space to begin with, more as your event history grows.
  • Processor: A modern dual-core or better.
  • Browser: Any current version of Edge, Chrome, or Firefox for viewing your dashboards.

Nowhere. candela runs on your own machine and stores everything locally. That is the entire point of a self-hosted analytics tool.
